This channel exists under the NDHSCCI 2023 Data Privacy Manual (Art. XIII §64–66) and Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Reports of suspected breaches are routed to the Data Breach Response Team per Manual Art. XI §51–52.

This is a private, confidential channel monitored only by the Office of the Data Protection Officer. For anything not related to data privacy, please contact the Registrar or Guidance Office directly.
