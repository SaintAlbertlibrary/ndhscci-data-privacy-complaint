Submit your report

Access and Correction requests follow the official forms in the NDHSCCI Data Privacy Manual (Annexes D and E) and download as a signable PDF. Suspected breaches are submitted directly online. This channel is not monitored for academic, disciplinary, or general school matters.

Looking for something else? Grade appeals, enrollment questions, and general concerns go through the Registrar or Guidance Office directly, not this form.

This channel exists under the NDHSCCI 2023 Data Privacy Manual (Art. XIII §64–66) and Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Reports of suspected breaches are routed to the Data Breach Response Team per Manual Art. XI §51–52.

This is a private, confidential channel monitored only by the Office of the Data Protection Officer. For anything not related to data privacy, please contact the Registrar or Guidance Office directly.
