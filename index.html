How it works

What happens after you submit

Every step is designed so you always know where your report stands, and who can see it.

1

You submit securely

Choose to share your name or stay completely anonymous. Either way, your report goes straight to the DPO — not to teachers, staff, or classmates.

2

The DPO reviews it privately

For a suspected breach, the Data Breach Response Team is notified immediately. For any report, only people with a direct need to help resolve it are involved.

3

You get a resolution

If you left contact details, expect a reply within 3 business days. Breach reports are acted on right away, whether or not you're reachable.

Your rights

What the law guarantees you

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), every data subject at NDHSCCI has these rights — this channel exists to make them usable.

Be informed

Know what personal data of yours is collected, why, and who can see it.

Access & correct

Request a copy of your records, and have inaccurate information fixed.

Request erasure

Ask that data be removed when it's no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.

File a complaint

Raise a concern about how your data was handled — this channel is that report line.

Ready to file a report?

It takes about two minutes. You can stay anonymous the entire time.

This channel exists under the NDHSCCI 2023 Data Privacy Manual (Art. XIII §64–66) and Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Reports of suspected breaches are routed to the Data Breach Response Team per Manual Art. XI §51–52.

This is a private, confidential channel monitored only by the Office of the Data Protection Officer. For anything not related to data privacy, please contact the Registrar or Guidance Office directly.
